Tax on the coupons

Gilt coupons are savings income; redemption is CGT-exempt and never taxed. The starting rate for savings and the Personal Savings Allowance make the rate a step function of how much coupon income each tax year carries, so the effective rate below is what gilt selection was actually priced against — not your marginal rate.

Why each rung

Rungs are picked by after-tax cost per £1 delivered. Backward cash-flow matching is not provably cost-minimal, so the runner-up for each rung is shown: a small gap means little was left on the table, a large one means the choice mattered.

If rates move

Each row is the whole ladder rebuilt against a shifted curve, not an estimate from duration — so it shows the ladder re-selecting different gilts, which an estimate cannot. A cash-flow matched ladder should be fairly insensitive; that is a claim worth checking.

Take it away
